Frequently asked questions

All FAQs
Is RoomSaathi really broker-free?

Yes. You deal directly with the owner and we never charge brokerage on rent.

What is a visit slot?

A time window when the owner can show you the room. Book one with a small refundable token to see the room before you decide.

When do I get my advance back?

Cancel at least 3 days before move-in for a full refund; if the owner cancels you are always refunded in full.
