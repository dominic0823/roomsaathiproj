About us

Rooms should be easy to find, and honest to rent.

RoomSaathi connects tenants with room owners directly. Students moving to a new city, families on transfer and defence personnel on posting often lose weeks - and a month's rent - to brokers. We built a simple, transparent alternative.

What we believe

  • No brokerage. Owners and tenants deal directly.
  • Trust by default. OTP-verified phones, verified-owner badges and reviews only from real stays.
  • Fair money rules. Small refundable tokens and advances, clear cancellation policy, payments via Razorpay.
